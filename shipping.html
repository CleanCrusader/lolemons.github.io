Policies

Shipping Policy

Free standard shipping on every order, tracked delivery, and a faster option if you need it. We ship within the United States.

Where we ship

We currently ship to addresses within the United States only. We don't ship internationally.

Shipping options and cost

  • Standard shipping — Free. Estimated delivery 3–5 business days after your order ships.
  • Expedited shipping — $9.99. Estimated delivery 1–2 business days after your order ships.

You choose your shipping option at checkout, before you pay. The price shown at checkout is the full shipping cost — there are no hidden fees. Sales tax, where applicable, is shown at checkout too.

Order processing

Orders are fulfilled from our US warehouse partner and typically ship within 3–5 business days of being placed. Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding US holidays. Orders placed on a weekend or holiday are processed the next business day.

Tracking

When your order ships, we email you a confirmation with a tracking number so you can follow it to your door.

Delays, lost or damaged packages

Carrier delays can happen, especially around holidays and bad weather. If your order hasn't arrived within the estimated window, or it arrives damaged, email contact@lolemons.com with your order number and we'll make it right. See our Returns & Refunds policy for details.

Questions?

Email contact@lolemons.com or call (321) 234-7119.
